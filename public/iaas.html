What is IaaS?

Infrastructure, delivered as a service.

Instead of buying, racking and maintaining physical servers, you provision exactly the compute, storage and networking you need — on demand. You pay for what you use, scale up or down at any time, and let us handle the hardware, the data center, and the operations.

You focus on your application. We handle the infrastructure.

Sham Cloud IaaS gives you everything a hyperscaler offers — virtual machines, block storage, snapshots, public IPs, load balancers, firewalls — backed by a team you can call in Damascus.
