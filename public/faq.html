Frequently asked questions

The difference lies in how resources are distributed and the level of continuity. Virtual servers share resources within a single physical server and are affected by hardware failure, while dedicated hosting relies on an interconnected network of servers — offering superior reliability, since if one machine fails, another automatically takes over to expand resources immediately.

Sham Cloud is built on redundant infrastructure with 24/7 monitoring, failover across nodes, and managed backup policies to keep your services running.

Shared hosting places multiple customers on the same physical server, sharing its CPU, RAM, and storage — a cost-effective option for lighter workloads.

Contact our team through the Contact Us page with your organization details and intended workload — we'll help you scope and provision a trial.

Local hosting keeps your data inside Syrian borders, reduces latency, aligns with local regulatory requirements, and gives you direct access to a locally supported team.

IaaS provides scalable compute, storage, and networking as an on-demand service, letting you build and operate your own environments on top of Sham Cloud's local infrastructure.
